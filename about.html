About Us

Free Social Media Image Resizer is a simple online tool built to help users resize images for popular social media platforms like WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

Many users struggle to find the correct image dimensions for profile pictures, posts, stories, and thumbnails. This tool removes that confusion by providing ready-made presets and flexible resize options.

What This Tool Offers

  • Predefined social media ratios
  • Fit, Fill, Blur, Space, and Stretch modes
  • Fast browser-based processing
  • No signup or login required

All image processing happens directly in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy and security.
